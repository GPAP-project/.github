# Global Pathogen Analysis Platform (GPAP) AI Research

We build agentic AI and AI-powered tools for the [Global Pathogen Analysis Platform (GPAP)](https://gpap.global): a free platform that helps scientists and public health authorities detect, track and analyze infectious diseases.

- **Team website:** https://gpap-project.github.io
- **Platform:** https://gpap.global
- **News:** https://gpap.global/news

GPAP is hosted at the DTU National Food Institute, with partner nodes at the University of Copenhagen, Statens Serum Institut and Imperial College London. It is funded by the Novo Nordisk Foundation.
